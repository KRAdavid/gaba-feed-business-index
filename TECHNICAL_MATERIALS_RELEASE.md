## Evidence policy

- GABA 20% is the provisional product target.
- Other numerical quality limits remain subject to commercial-lot analytics, validated methods, and target-market feed regulations.
- Growth, feed efficiency, reproductive performance, methane reduction, and carbon-credit claims are not fixed before controlled validation.
